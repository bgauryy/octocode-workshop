The workshop. Your turn to research with Octocode, and every "done" is a citation: file:line, a version or a PR. The guided exercises are being rebuilt. For now this is a short practice prompt: pick one question about your own repository, use search and structure to find candidates, fetch the deciding lines, and cite the file and line. Pair work is welcome. After the exercise, return to three gotchas the research flow helps prevent, then the design rules for tools and skills.
