LLM basics

Each token gets a key, value and query

Attention works like a search engine: the query searches, keys get matched, values come back.

Example The animal didn't cross the street because it was tired.

Q and K decide where to look · V decides what you take. Q = X·W_Q   K = X·W_K   V = X·W_V
Think of a search engine. Click 1, the search bar: that's the Query. The token "it" in "The animal didn't cross the street because it was tired" is asking: what am I looking for, what do I refer to? Click 2, the Keys: every token has a searchable title, what it offers for matching. The query is compared with every key; "animal" is the top match, "street" is a weak one. Click 3, the Values: the result content, the information each token actually contributes. "it" takes mostly from "animal" and very little from "street". Why three separate vectors? Because finding relevant information and providing it are different jobs: what makes a token easy to find is not the same as what it should hand over. Click 4: Q and K decide where to look; V decides what you take. All three come from the same token embedding X, multiplied by three learned matrices: W_Q, W_K and W_V. Source: Vaswani et al. 2017, "Attention Is All You Need", arXiv:1706.03762. Hand-off: next, watch the search run on one sentence.
