The catch: thinking is billed as output tokens, and it only reasons over what it's given.

Definition: reasoning is the model breaking a problem into steps (decompose, work, check) before it commits to an answer, instead of recalling text. Those steps are extra tokens the model writes first; you usually only see the final answer. OpenAI: "Reasoning models use internal reasoning tokens before producing a response" (developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning); Anthropic: Claude "thinks against that budget before it starts its final answer" (platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/extended-thinking). Click 1, how it got here. 2022, a prompt trick: Wei et al., chain-of-thought prompting, Jan 2022: on GSM8K math with PaLM 540B, accuracy rose from 17.9% to 56.9%; the paper says "performance more than doubled for the largest GPT and PaLM models" (arXiv:2201.11903, Tables 2 and 3). Kojima et al., May 2022: just adding "Let's think step by step" took text-davinci-002 on GSM8K from 10.4% to 40.7% (arXiv:2205.11916). 2024 on, trained into the model: OpenAI o1, 12 Sep 2024: on AIME 2024 o1 scored 74% with one sample vs GPT-4o's 12% (openai.com, Learning to reason with LLMs). DeepSeek-R1, 22 Jan 2025: reasoning learned "through pure reinforcement learning (RL)" (arXiv:2501.12948). Claude 3.7 Sonnet, 24 Feb 2025: "the first hybrid reasoning model generally available on the market", answer fast or think longer (anthropic.com/news/claude-3-7-sonnet). Today it's built in and on by default: on Claude Opus 5.5 thinking is on by default and can't be turned off (platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/thinking); GPT-6 Astra (released Sep 2026) and GPT-6.1 Sol can't set reasoning effort to "none" (developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model); checked Oct 2026. So you no longer prompt for chain of thought. You tune how much of it you get. API knobs: Claude API uses thinking: {type: "adaptive"} plus output_config effort (the older thinking: {type: "enabled", budget_tokens: N} is deprecated on 4.6 models and errors on 4.7+); OpenAI API uses reasoning: {effort: …}, values depend on the model. Click 2, the catch: thinking is billed as output tokens and fills the same window. Anthropic: "You are billed for the full thinking process, not the thinking content visible in the response" (platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/thinking-steering-and-cost); OpenAI: reasoning tokens "occupy space in the model's context window and are billed as output tokens". And it reasons over whatever evidence it has: it amplifies, it doesn't verify. The trace isn't proof either: "Claude 3.7 Sonnet mentioned the hint 25% of the time, and DeepSeek R1 mentioned it 39% of the time" (anthropic.com/research/reasoning-models-dont-say-think, Apr 2025). More thinking isn't always better: longer reasoning "deteriorates performance" on some tasks (arXiv:2507.14417). That's why Octocode's tools take optional mainGoal and reasoning fields: the model says why it calls a tool (octocode-core src/toolContract/input/resources/global.ts:16-22, mainGoalSchema and reasoningSchema; the same descriptions appear in packages/octocode-config/contract/tool_types.rs of the dev tree; ledger V5).
