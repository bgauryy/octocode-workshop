From the brief: "I needed to search a lot of code in GitHub; this is how Octocode was created; started as research loop." The pain: large repos and many repos, public GitHub, organizations. The comic is the real moment: "find all references to our project and all usages of our internal logic, internally and externally", then hours of manual searching. Agents at the time would read one or two files and then confidently guess: sycophancy again, now with code. Click 4, the birth certificate: octocode-mcp 1.0.0 was published to npm on 2025-06-06 (npm view octocode-mcp time, run 2026-10-09; first commit "Initial commit" 2025-06-05). It shipped 14 snake_case tools: search_github_code, fetch_github_file_content, view_repository_structure, search_github_repos / commits / pull_requests / issues / discussions / topics / users, get_user_organizations, npm_search, npm_view, plus view_repository, the 14th (36f55ae3c:src/tools/contstants.ts:2-15). GitHub calls went through the gh CLI, and results sat in an in-memory cache with a 1-hour TTL (36f55ae3c:src/impl.ts:27-28; still 1 h in the published 1.0.1; raised to 24 h in cc85f5f96 later that day, 6 Jun 2025). Before the rename the project was called "Colombus MCP" (b2509f856). So Octocode's first shape was a loop over live tools, not an index.
