"Context engineering is the delicate art and science of filling the context window with just the right information for the next step."
Andrej Karpathy, June 2025
Let the quote land. Credit where it's due: Tobi Lütke used the term first, on 19 Jun 2025: "I really like the term 'context engineering' over prompt engineering … the art of providing all the context for the task to be plausibly solvable by the LLM" (twitter.com/tobi/status/1935533422589399127). Karpathy, 25 Jun 2025 (x.com/karpathy/status/1937902205765607626), replying to Lütke: "+1 for context engineering over prompt engineering… the delicate art and science of filling the context window with just the right information for the next step." Sycophancy, ambiguity, schemas, attention: all the same root, what's in the window. Octocode is my answer to "just the right information" for code.
