August 2026 campaign: 30 public GitHub questions × 3 runs, seed 42, a fresh agent per question, blind gpt-5.5 judge (packages/octocode-benchmark/results/SUMMARY.md:20-41; ledger X8). The questions are public: https://github.com/bgauryy/octocode/tree/main/packages/octocode-benchmark/compare/github-questions — open one so people see what "a question" means. The table is my results screenshot: mean characters per question (model in + out): Octocode v18.1.1 CLI 22,111, plain gh 114,849, gh + RTK 368,586, gh + Headroom 382,598. The "vs Octocode" column is the per-question ratio (geometric mean), not the ratio of the means: 2.01× for gh, 3.22× for RTK, 2.73× for Headroom. (SUMMARY.md reports 1.99× / 3.21× / 2.62× with 95% CIs 1.52–2.61, 2.36–4.46, 1.87–3.71 — say "about 2 to 3.2×".) Press →: correctness was a tie — 9.19 vs 9.27 against gh, 9.29 vs 9.42 against RTK, 9.30 vs 8.62 against Headroom; MATRIX.md treats arms within one point as equally correct. Compressing output after the fact (RTK, Headroom) used more characters, not fewer, because the agent kept asking for more. Caveat: this was the v18.1.1 CLI, not today's v20, and plain gh with no guidance is an easy opponent. Press →: the link to the question set. Next: in October I made the opponent as strong as I could.
