The problem: prompt rules conflict, duplicate each other, or never reach the model at all. This skill traces each instruction through its loader or tool to the observable action, then fixes the surface that owns it. Walk the pipe: Understand intent and reader (goal, scope, permissions, success signal); Inspect the rules and the effective context; then review (report) or write and improve by editing the smallest owning surface; Check intent, boundaries and output, looping on a gap; Report (skills/octocode-agentic-prompts/SKILL.md:10-19). Press → for the rule: "For assembled prompts, inspect the effective model or tool input before judging duplication or size. A rule in source may be missing or repeated at runtime." (SKILL.md:24). Issues are prioritized by effect: unsafe or conflicting instructions, then unclear decisions and outputs, then redundancy and wording (:25). Extra: "Put types and limits in schemas, selection guidance in descriptions, and cross-tool order in the workflow" (:32); "Labels help readability; they do not create authority or replace permission checks" (:36); behavioral gains stay unmeasured until evaluated (:50). References cover tool contracts, agent communication, context budget, token economics and untrusted content (:54-63).
