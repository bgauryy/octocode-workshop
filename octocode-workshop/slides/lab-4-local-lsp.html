Lab 4: prove who calls a function

Use language-server relationships to distinguish symbol identity from text matches.

# map → search → prove (prefix: npx) octocode structureSearch '{"queries":[{ "operation":"tree", "path":"src"}]}' octocode localSearch '{"queries":[{ "path":"src", "matchString":"loadConfig", "resultView":"matchOnly"}]}' octocode lspSearch '{"queries":[{ "path":"src/config.ts", "lineHint":12, "operation":"references", "symbolName":"loadConfig"}]}'
prompt your agent"I want to change loadConfig's signature. Who breaks? Prove each caller with LSP, not text matches."
done when

Your agent separates real callers from same-named text hits — and lists the files that break.

Lesson: the same contract locally, and "text hits are not identity" (instructions.ts). Use any TypeScript repo of your own; replace loadConfig, path and lineHint with a real function (the starter repo, when created, plants a same-name symbol so grep over-counts).
