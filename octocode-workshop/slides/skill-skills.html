The problem: skills fail silently. They don't trigger, they link to files that aren't there, or their lobby is bloated. This skill is the authoring standard for the whole collection, plus a linter and installer guidance. Walk the pipe: Understand the request; Inspect the skill and its files; Review structure, triggers and docs; then by outcome: review reports findings and readiness (verdict Ready or Needs work), create or improve edits the owning files, install or sync inspects destinations and applies; Verify changed behavior and links, looping back on an unresolved defect (skills/octocode-skills/SKILL.md:10-22). Press → for the rule: "Check activation against realistic requests with varied phrasing, implicit intent, and near-misses that belong to neighboring skills. Fix the intent boundary rather than adding exact keyword exceptions." (SKILL.md:53). Keep three verdicts apart: "Lint checks files and structure. Editorial review checks meaning. Host activation tests check actual selection." (:63). Extra: scripts/skill-review.mjs is the structural lint (with --self-test to check the checker) and scripts/skill-sync.mjs links a local skill into vendor folders (SKILL.md:51, :72-73); this skill also owns the canonical render-report.mjs that every other skill copies byte-identical (skills/README.md:7). Invoke: npx octocode skill install octocode-skills, then, for example (illustrative prompt): "Review my octocode-slides skill and make it trigger on 'make me a talk'." Hand-off: description and trigger wording → octocode-agentic-prompts; prose → octocode-documentation; measured activation claims → octocode-eval-benchmark (SKILL.md:79-82).
