Every Octocode tool result carries hints, so the agent knows what to do next instead of guessing. Click 1: the agent searches and gets nothing back. Click 2: the result itself says what to try, for example "try wider filters". A real one from the Rust runtime is "Nothing searched: include/exclude matched no file." (local_search/leads.rs:41, ledger V8). It works like a compiler that says "did you mean…?" instead of just "error". Click 3: the same idea in all three cases. Found something: next-page channels (31 kinds, such as nextPage and continueWindow) and ready-to-run {tool, query} leads like read and findCallers (48 kinds) (packages/octocode-config/contract/tool-contract.json; docs/OCTOCODE_PROTOCOL.md:88-93; ledger V7). Found nothing: a short hint, at most 120 characters (MAX_GUIDANCE_CHARS, crates/runtime/src/response/rows.rs:44). Failed: one of 120 typed error codes, each with the fix in words, e.g. "Run octocode auth login or set GH_TOKEN/GITHUB_TOKEN…" (gh_shared/mod.rs, ledger V8). Outside support: Anthropic's "Writing effective tools for agents" (11 Sep 2025) says to "prompt-engineer your error responses". Remember: empty does not mean absent. Next: your turn.
