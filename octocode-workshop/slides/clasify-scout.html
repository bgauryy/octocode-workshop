Walk the bars. For the same ast-grep question, reading all 54 files returned 644 KB to the agent. Search hits alone returned 58 KB. Scout over all 54 files returned 11.8 KB in 2 calls and 3.4 seconds, warm. Adding fileHas:["template"] first narrowed to 9 files: 1 call, 1.9 KB, 0.7 seconds, with the answer ranked first. That is about 6× fewer bytes and 5× faster than scouting all 54 (derived: 11.8/1.9, 3.4/0.7). Every resource-by-question cell is another Jev judgment, and Jev's tokens are billed by the classification provider, separately from the host model, so they are not in these bytes. These are bytes returned to the agent, not provider tokens. Adoption caveat: in one A/B run, clasify was offered in 30 sessions and called 0 times (ledger X10). Scout is beta (X39).
