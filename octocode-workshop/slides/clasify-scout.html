Walk the bars. For the same ast-grep question, reading all 54 files returned 644 KB to the agent. Search hits alone returned 58 KB. Scout over all 54 files returned 11.8 KB in 2 calls and 3.4 seconds, warm. Adding fileHas:["template"] first narrowed to 9 files: 1 call, 1.9 KB, 0.7 seconds, with the answer ranked first. That is about 6× fewer bytes and 5× less time than scouting all 54 (derived: 11.8/1.9, 3.4/0.7). My point: when I tested more approaches — more files, more questions — it cost more tokens. Every file-by-question cell is a judgment, and Jev's provider tokens are billed by the classification provider, separately from the host model, so they are not in these bytes. These are bytes returned to the agent, not total provider tokens. If you know a literal name, just search it.
