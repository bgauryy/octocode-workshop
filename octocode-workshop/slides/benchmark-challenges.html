Open: benchmarking is tricky, and the easiest person to fool is the author. Every shortcut makes the numbers flatter the tool. Five traps, and the guard I use against each. Press 1 (weak baseline): Kapoor et al. 2024, "AI Agents That Matter" (arXiv:2407.01502), found agent benchmarks undermined by weak baselines, accuracy-only reporting and missing holdouts. So I benchmark against the strongest opponent I can build, and it hurts: in October gh-lean beat Octocode on characters (0.71×). Press 2 (tuning on the test set): Goodhart's law, paraphrased: when a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure. In August I added generic rules after Q21 and Q25 failed, so "those two questions are no longer clean tests" (method.md:42-54). The method says: "The set is public and may be memorized… use private held-out questions for shipping decisions" (method.md:91-92). Honest gap: the private set isn't built yet. Press 3 (leaks): Zhou et al. (arXiv:2311.01964) show contamination inflates scores. compare/bin/leak_check.py takes each question's distinctive terms and fails the run if any appear in the arm briefs or in the live MCP initialize instructions and tools/list. Press 4 (one run, one number): Anthropic, "A statistical approach to model evals" (anthropic.com/research/statistical-approach-to-model-evals): report standard error and 95% confidence intervals, and use paired questions. August used 3 runs and 10k bootstrap resamples. October shows why every metric matters: Octocode lost on characters (0.71×) but won on time, median 16.5 s vs 19.4 s, made 80 vs 142 calls, at about the same cost, $2.61 vs $2.49 (results/lean-vs-mcp-114934-2026-10-09.md:48-57). Reproducibility: refs frozen to SHAs, empty working dir. Press 5 (trusting the judge): Zheng et al. 2023 (arXiv:2306.05685) document position, verbosity and self-preference bias in LLM judges. The guard: a blind, shuffled judge, spot-checked against humans. Ours today: gpt-5.5 as judge, with tool names redacted. Close: the public set is for iterating, the private set is for shipping.
