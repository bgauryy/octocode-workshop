Three rules before we build

Recap the LLM mechanics and failure modes, then bring your questions.

01 · the loop

Your output is the next prompt.

The model re-reads everything, every turn — and believes it.

02 · tokens + attention

Small and sharp beats big.

Tokens cost money and attention; the middle gets lost.

03 · caching

Stable first, volatile last.

Cache reads cost 0.1× — for an identical prefix only. Anthropic prompt caching docs

Checkpoint before the Octocode story. Each rule redraws the picture from the slide that taught it. (1) The loop: agents decide from what tools return, and the window is re-sent every turn — your output is the next prompt. (2) The U-curve: tokens cost money and attention, and facts in the middle get lost (Liu et al. 2023; ledger B6) — small and sharp beats big. (3) The prefix bar: provider caches bill only an identical prefix at the discount (ledger B7, B8) — stable first, volatile last. Click 4: the 2023 lesson — sycophancy and FlipFlop (ledger H3, H4) mean the model will please before it proves, so tools must demand evidence. Take 3–4 minutes of questions.
