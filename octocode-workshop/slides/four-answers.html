The question first: it sounds like trivia with one answer. Click 1: four real screenshots, four models (the speaker's own screenshots, 2025; ledger H2). Click 2: the top answer from each, readable from the back. ChatGPT and Grok say Gregg Popovich (at 71 and 70), Gemini 2.5 Pro and Perplexity say Hubie Brown (at 70 and 71); Perplexity even ranks Hubie Brown at 71 first and then lists Popovich at 73 second, contradicting its own ranking. The reveal: "oldest" hides several records (oldest to coach a playoff game, oldest in the Finals, oldest to win a title), so each model confidently picked a different lane (sources: HoopsHype; Wikipedia, Gregg Popovich and Hubie Brown). Why confidently instead of asking? OpenAI and Georgia Tech: "language models hallucinate because the training and evaluation procedures reward guessing over acknowledging uncertainty" (arXiv:2509.04664, 4 Sep 2025). Ambiguous context in, confident garbage out. This is the whole workshop in one slide: define the question, and the disagreement disappears. Next: what I did about both failures.
